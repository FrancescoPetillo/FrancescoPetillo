### About Me

I'm a curious and motivated person who has always loved technology and understanding how things work.

I have strong foundations in computer science and a genuine passion for operating systems and computer architecture. I love exploring what happens under the hood, from firmware and the boot process to system buses, memory, and hardware components.

I'm also fascinated by how systems communicate, how networks are built, and how security mechanisms protect the infrastructures we rely on every day. I enjoy learning, experimenting, breaking things in my lab, and building a deeper understanding of technology rather than simply using it.

- 🎓 BSc in Computer Science and Cybersecurity — University of Naples Parthenope
- 🎓 MSc student in Cybersecurity and Cloud Technologies — University of Salerno
- 🐧 Linux enthusiast and open-source supporter
- 💻 Building projects with Python, Docker and open-source technologies

### Let's Connect

I'm always looking to meet new people who share my curiosity for technology, regardless of their experience or background.

I'd love to exchange ideas, challenge each other's perspectives, learn from people who know more than I do, and share what I know with those who are just getting started.

Living in Naples, I sometimes find it difficult to meet people with similar interests, so I'm particularly open to connecting with people from anywhere in the world.

Whether it's a professional opportunity, an open-source project, a technical discussion, or simply experimenting with something new for fun, I'm always happy to collaborate and to made new friends.
